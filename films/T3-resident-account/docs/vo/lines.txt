Your estate uses BreeUp for bills, notices and visitor passes. There's no app to download, and setting up takes about two minutes.
First, open the sign-in link your estate admin sent you. You'll see your estate's name at the top.
Type your phone number, any way you like.
Then enter the temporary four-digit PIN your admin gave you.
Now choose your own PIN: four digits only you know, typed twice. Skip easy ones, like your house number.
And you're in. Your Home page shows what you owe, your latest notices, and the visitor pass card.
Tip: add the page to your home screen, and it opens like any other app.
