Paying your estate service charge takes about a minute, and you get a receipt.
First, open your estate's sign-in link, and sign in with your phone number and four-digit PIN.
Next, open Bills. You'll see what you owe, and when it's due.
To pay, transfer from any Nigerian bank app to the estate account shown on the page.
Add your unit reference to the narration, so the payment lands against your home.
Want fewer reminders? Pay a few months ahead, and you won't be billed for them again.
One last thing. Estates rarely change their bank account. If you're asked to pay into a new one, call a committee member first.
