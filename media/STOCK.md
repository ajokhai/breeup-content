# Stock footage to download

The build machine can't reach stock sites, so these need downloading by hand.
Pexels is free for commercial use with no attribution. Pick clips with Nigerian or West African people
and settings; skip anything that obviously reads as another region.

Download the **HD 1920x1080** (or 4K) MP4 and save it into `Subby/videos/media/stock/` with the file name in the
first column. Portrait clips are a bonus for the 9:16 cuts but not required: I crop landscape clips
for mobile.

## Needed for T1 (visitor pass), and reused across most films

| Save as | What to look for | Search |
|---|---|---|
| `gate-car.mp4` | A car pulling up to a gated estate or compound gate, gate opening | [pexels: gate car](https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/gate%20car%20africa/) |
| `guard-phone.mp4` | Security guard at a gate checking a phone, or talking to a driver | [pexels: security guard africa](https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/security%20guard%20african/) |
| `woman-phone-home.mp4` | African woman at home typing on her phone, relaxed | [pexels: african woman phone](https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/african%20woman%20phone/) |
| `man-phone.mp4` | African man reading or typing on his phone, indoors or on a porch | [pexels: african man phone](https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/african%20man%20phone/) |
| `delivery-rider.mp4` | Delivery rider or courier arriving with a package | [pexels: delivery africa](https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/delivery%20man%20african/) |

the films already have the site's own estate photos to fall back on.
