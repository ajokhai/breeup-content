# Social media plan (starter)

Goal for the first 3 months: be findable and credible when a committee member hears about BreeUp, and get
estates to set up or contact us. Followers matter less than enquiries.

## Channels, in priority order

| Channel | Why | Who we reach | Post |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp Channel + Status** | Where estates already live. Committee members forward things. | Committees, residents | Every post, plus tutorials when an estate onboards |
| **Facebook** (Page) | Older committee members and estate groups are here | Committees, landlords | 3x a week |
| **Instagram** | Reels reach; credibility check for younger residents | Residents, younger committee members | 3-4x a week (Reels first) |
| **LinkedIn** (company page + Josh's profile) | Facility managers, developers, property managers | Buyers and partners | 2x a week |
| **YouTube** | Home for tutorials (searchable, embeddable on the blog), Shorts for reels | Everyone searching "how to" | Every tutorial + every reel as a Short |
| **TikTok** | Cheap reach with reels | Residents, younger landlords | 3x a week once reels exist |

Skip X for now unless Josh wants it.

## What we post (content pillars)

1. **How-to** (tutorials T1-T7): short, useful, evergreen. Cut each into a 15-30 s reel (S-series).
2. **Problem and fix** (explainers E1-E4): the WhatsApp group that stopped working, chasing service charge,
   a busy gatehouse, a tense AGM. Speak to committees.
3. **Trust**: how estate money is held (own account, licensed banking partner, second approver), pricing
   explained (free setup, estate keeps every naira). Plain carousel posts work well here.
4. **Estate life**: short, warm moments that look like our customers' lives (generated by vid-gen).
5. **People** (when real): Josh, the team, real estates using BreeUp, with permission. Never invented.

Rough mix: 40% how-to, 25% problem and fix, 20% trust, 15% estate life and people.

## Each week, record (in this file, bottom section)

Reach, saves/shares, link clicks, profile visits, enquiries (DMs, emails, set-ups). After 4 weeks, double down on
the two post types with the most enquiries and drop the weakest.
