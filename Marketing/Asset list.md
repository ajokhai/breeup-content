## Ready-made captions

**S1:** Service charge, sorted. Residents pay by card or transfer from their phone, the payment lands against their home, and the
committee sees it straight away. Set up your estate free at breeup.com

**T1:** Expecting a guest? Send them a visitor pass on WhatsApp in under a minute. The guard checks the code at
the gate. Ask your estate committee about BreeUp.

**T2:** Paying your service charge shouldn't take a trip to the bank. Pay by card or transfer in about a minute,
with a receipt every time.

**T3:** New to BreeUp? Set up your resident account with your phone number and a 4-digit PIN. It takes less
than a minute.

**T4:** For estate treasurers: bill every household, send reminders automatically and see who has paid at a
glance. No more chasing on WhatsApp. Set up free at breeup.com

## How to use the tutorials in sales

Tutorials double as proof that the product is simple. Send T4 to a committee after a first conversation, and T2
and T3 to an estate that has just signed up, to forward to residents.
