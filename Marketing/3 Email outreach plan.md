# Email outreach plan (starter)

Goal: conversations with estates and partners that lead to set-ups. Small, careful, personal sends beat
volume. Start with 20-40 well-researched contacts a week.

## Who to contact

| Segment | Who exactly | Where to find them (legitimately) | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Estate committees** | Chairman, secretary, treasurer of residents' associations in Lekki, Ajah, Ikoyi, Magodo, Gbagada, Gwarinpa, Lugbe, Katampe... | Estate websites and Facebook pages, published association contacts, referrals from residents, introductions at estate events | Free setup, a call to walk through it, T4 video |
| **Facility management companies** | Directors and operations heads | Company websites, LinkedIn, facility management association member lists, industry events | Partner programme: their brand, they set what estates pay, they earn on payments |
| **Developers** | Sales, handover and after-sales leads | Company websites, LinkedIn, property expos | Estate set up for handover; residents onboarded from day one |
| **Landlords and multiplex owners** (incl. diaspora) | Owners of compounds and small blocks | Referrals, landlord associations, LinkedIn | See rent and charges paid from anywhere |

Keep a sheet with: name, role, organisation, email, **where we got it**, segment, date sent, replies, next step,
opt-out. "Where we got it" matters for the law below.

## Privacy and the law (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)

- Only email **work contacts** for **work reasons**, with details they published themselves or gave us.
  No bought lists, no scraping personal data.
- Say who we are and why we're writing in the first line. Keep a real sender (a person at breeup.com).
- Every email has an easy opt-out ("Reply 'no thanks' and I won't write again"). Honour it within 2 days and
  record it.
- Store the sheet somewhere private; delete contacts who opt out or don't reply after the sequence.
- When in doubt, ask legal@breeup.com.

## Sequence for estate committees (4 emails, about 2 weeks)

**Email 1 (day 1). Subject: Service charge for [Estate name]**
> Good morning [Name],
>
> I'm [your name] from BreeUp. We help estate committees collect service charge without chasing residents:
> bills and reminders go out on their own, residents pay online against their unit, and you can see who has
> paid at a glance. The money goes into the estate's own account, and the estate keeps every naira it bills.
>
> Would a 15-minute call next week be useful? I can show you how it would work for [Estate name].
>
> [Name], BreeUp · breeup.com
> (If this isn't for you, reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.)

**Email 2 (day 4). Subject: Re: Service charge for [Estate name]**
> A one-minute video showing how a treasurer bills residents and sends reminders: [T4 link]. Setup is free and
> takes about five minutes. Happy to do it with you on a call.

**Email 3 (day 8). Subject: Who holds the estate's money?**
> The question committees ask us most. Each estate has its own account with our licensed banking partner and pays
> out to its own bank; large payouts can need a second approver, and everything shows up in the AGM report.
> Neither BreeUp nor anyone else holds estate funds. Worth a short call?

**Email 4 (day 14). Subject: Closing the loop**
> I won't keep writing. If service charge collection or the gatehouse becomes a headache later, we're at
> hello@breeup.com. Wishing [Estate name] a smooth year.

## Sequence for facility managers (3 emails)

1. **"Run estate management under your own brand"**: the partner programme; they set estate pricing and earn on
   payments; their clients keep their own accounts. Ask for a call.
2. **Proof it's simple**: T4 and T2 links; setup in minutes per estate; no hardware.
3. **Close the loop** politely, with the opt-out.

## Tools

Start with Gmail or Google Workspace and a sheet; move to a sending tool (with tracking and automatic opt-outs)
only after the first 100 contacts. Warm up any new sending domain slowly. Never send more than about 50 a day
from one inbox.

## What to measure

Reply rate (aim for 10%+), calls booked, set-ups. Note which subject lines and segments reply most at the
bottom of this file, and rewrite the weak emails every month.
