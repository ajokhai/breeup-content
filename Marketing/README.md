## Ground rules for anyone marketing BreeUp

1. **Josh approves anything public** (posts, emails, ads) until he says otherwise.
2. **Only true claims.** No invented customers, numbers or testimonials. The demo estate in our videos
   ("Greenview Estate") is a demo; never present it as a customer.
3. **Plain English**, no pidgin or slang, readable by a 60-year-old committee member.
4. **AI-generated people:** our videos use generated photos and clips. Tick each platform's "AI-generated"
   or "altered content" label where its rules require it (Meta, TikTok and YouTube all have one).
5. **Privacy:** follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 for any contact list (details in the email plan).
6. **Track everything** with the UTM convention in the ads plan, so we know what brings estates in.
7. When you learn what works, write it into the relevant plan file. These files are meant to improve over time.
