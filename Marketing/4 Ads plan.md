# Ads plan (starter)

Goal: estates setting up or contacting us, at a cost we can afford. Ads go to the people who decide (committees,
facility managers, landlords), not residents, until we see what converts.

## Platforms

1. **Meta (Facebook + Instagram)**: first and biggest. Older committee members are on Facebook; good targeting
   by location and interests; lead forms work on mobile.
2. **YouTube**: tutorials and explainers as in-stream ads; people searching "estate management", "service
   charge" or "visitor management".
3. **LinkedIn**: facility managers and developers only (expensive, so small and targeted).
4. **TikTok**: test later with reels, for landlords and younger committee members.
5. **Google Search** (not video): "estate management software Nigeria", "service charge collection app".
   Small budget, high intent.

## Audiences (Meta)

- **Committees:** Lagos (Lekki, Ajah, Ikoyi, VI, Magodo, Gbagada, Ikeja GRA), Abuja (Gwarinpa, Lugbe, Katampe,
  Life Camp, Asokoro), Port Harcourt. Ages 35-65. Interests: residents' associations, property management, real
  estate, homeowners. Exclude under 30.
- **Landlords incl. diaspora:** Nigerians living in the UK, US, Canada and Ireland, interested in Nigerian real
  estate or property investment.
- **Facility managers:** LinkedIn by job title and industry; on Meta, interest in facility management.
- **Retargeting** (once there's traffic): website visitors and video viewers (50%+) from the last 30 days.

## Creative (the A-series, being made in `vid-gen/`)

| ID | Ad | Audience | Lengths | Shapes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | "Still chasing service charge on WhatsApp?" | Committees | 15 s, 30 s | 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 |
| A2 | "The estate keeps every naira it bills" (trust) | Committees, treasurers | 15 s | 9:16, 4:5 |
| A3 | "A calmer gatehouse" (visitor passes) | Committees, security leads | 15 s | 9:16, 4:5 |
| A4 | "See who has paid, from anywhere" | Diaspora landlords | 15 s, 30 s | 9:16, 4:5 |
| A5 | "Run estates under your own brand" (partners) | Facility managers | 30 s | 16:9, 1:1 |
| A6 | 6-second bumpers (one line + logo) | Retargeting | 6 s | 9:16, 1:1 |

Each ad: the hook in the first 1.5 s, captions burned in (most people watch muted), one message, end card with
"Set up your estate free at breeup.com". Two hook variants per ad to test (three at most: each one costs render and picture credits). Until the A-series lands,
use S1 and T4 (phone cut).

## Budget and testing (starting point; adjust with real numbers)

- **Weeks 1-2 (test):** 3 ad sets (committees Lagos, committees Abuja, diaspora landlords) x 2-3 creatives each.
  Spread a fixed test budget evenly; Josh sets the amount. Judge on cost per lead and cost per landing-page
  view, not likes.
- **Weeks 3-4 (focus):** turn off the bottom half; move budget to the best audience-creative pairs; add
  retargeting.
- **Month 2+:** scale what produces set-ups; refresh creative every 3-4 weeks (ad fatigue).

## Where ads send people

- Committees: breeup.com/solutions/gated-communities (or /pricing for trust ads)
- Facility managers: breeup.com/solutions/facility-managers
- Landlords: /solutions/landlords-multiplex and /solutions/diaspora-landlords
- Lead forms (Meta) for committees: name, estate name, role, phone, number of homes.

## Tracking (UTM convention, use on every link, ads and organic)

`?utm_source=<facebook|instagram|youtube|linkedin|tiktok|google|whatsapp|email>&utm_medium=<paid|organic|email>&utm_campaign=<a1-chasing|t4-billing|...>&utm_content=<hook-a|hook-b>`

Install the Meta pixel and Google tag on breeup.com (website team) before spending, with "set up estate" and
"contact" as conversions.
