# Brand and messaging

## What BreeUp is (one line)

Estate management for Nigerian communities: dues billed and collected online, visitor passes on WhatsApp,
notices that reach the right homes, and records ready for the AGM.

## Who we sell to

The **buyer** (who signs up and pays the subscription) is usually not the resident. Market to buyers first.

| Audience | Who they are | What they want | Lead with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Estate committees (EXCOs)** | Chairman, secretary, treasurer of a residents' association | Dues paid on time without chasing; fewer arguments; a clean AGM | "Collect service charge without chasing residents." |
| **Facility managers / property managers** | Companies running several estates or buildings | Less admin per estate, a tool they can offer clients, new revenue | Partner programme: run BreeUp under your own brand and earn on payments. |
| **Developers** | Building and handing over new estates | A professional handover; residents set up from day one | "Hand over an estate that runs itself." |
| **Landlords, multiplexes, diaspora landlords** | Own a compound or a few flats, often from abroad | Rent and charges paid and visible without being there | "See who has paid, from anywhere." |
| **Residents** (users, not buyers) | People living in the estate | Paying easily, letting visitors in fast | Convenience: "Pay your service charge from your phone." |
| **Security leads / guards** | Gatehouse | Knowing who is expected | "Guards check a code in seconds." |

Website pages per audience: breeup.com/solutions/gated-communities, /facility-managers, /developers,
/landlords-multiplex, /diaspora-landlords, /mini-estates. Pricing: breeup.com/pricing.

## Key messages (all true, use freely)

- **Pay by card or bank transfer, in about a minute.** Residents tap "Pay online" for a secure checkout (card or
  transfer), or transfer straight to the estate account. Lead with card: it's what most people expect.
- **Collect without chasing.** Bills go out automatically, reminders follow on their own, and you can see who
  has paid at a glance.
- **The estate keeps every naira.** If the estate bills ₦25,000, it receives ₦25,000. A small payment fee is
  added on top for the resident, shown before they pay.
- **The estate's money stays the estate's.** Each estate has its own account with our licensed banking partner
  and pays out to its own bank. Neither BreeUp nor a partner holds estate funds. Large payouts can need a
  second approver.
- **Visitor passes on WhatsApp.** Create a pass in two taps; the visitor gets it on WhatsApp; the guard checks
  it on their phone; every entry is on record. The guard still has the final say.
- **Notices that reach the right homes**, on WhatsApp and in the resident portal, kept on record.
- **AGM-ready.** Reports and exports ready when the meeting is.
- **Free to set up**, about five minutes, no hardware. Subscription is based on the number of homes, quoted
  upfront. Residents never pay a subscription.
- **Your people stay in charge.** Software keeps the record; the executives make the calls.

## Never say

- **Auto-pay** (saved cards, bills paid automatically): not live yet. Don't mention it until the team posts
  `AUTO-PAY LIVE` in `../vid-gen/HANDOFF.md`. **Pay ahead is live** and fine to promote.
- Prices or discounts not on the pricing page (we quote per estate).
- "Bank" for BreeUp (we work through a licensed banking partner).
- Customer names, numbers of estates, money collected, or testimonials, unless Josh confirms they're real and
  approved for use.
- That we replace the guard, the committee or the AGM.

## Voice

Calm, plain and respectful, like a capable neighbour who sorts things out. Short sentences. Nigerian context
(naira, estates, EXCO, AGM, WhatsApp, gatehouse) without slang or pidgin. No hype words ("revolutionary",
"game-changing"). Address committees as busy volunteers, not as tech buyers.

## Calls to action

- Buyers: **"Set up your estate free at breeup.com"** or **"Talk to us: hello@breeup.com"**.
- Partners: **"Become a BreeUp partner"** (breeup.com, partner/white-label pages).
- Residents: **"Ask your estate committee about BreeUp."**
