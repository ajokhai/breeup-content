# YouTube plan and ready-to-paste copy

YouTube is our searchable video library: people type "how to pay service charge online" or "estate
management software Nigeria", and the blog embeds these videos. Upload the **wide** cut as the main video
and the **phone** cut (or the S-series reel) as a Short that links back to it.

## Channel setup (once)

- Name: **BreeUp**. Handle: @breeup (or closest available). Banner: green with the logo and "Estate management for
  Nigerian communities". Profile picture: `Brand/breeup-logo.svg` exported to PNG.
- About: the one-liner from `1 Brand and messaging.md`, plus breeup.com and hello@breeup.com.
- Links in header: breeup.com, breeup.com/pricing.
- Country: Nigeria. Default upload language: English.
- Playlists: **For residents** (T1, T2, T3), **For estate committees and admins** (T4-T7), **Why BreeUp**
  (E1-E4, L1), **Shorts**.
- Channel trailer: L1 (launch film) once it exists; until then T4.

## Upload checklist (every video)

1. File: the wide cut from `../Final videos/`. Rename it before upload to the SEO slug, e.g.
   `how-to-pay-estate-service-charge-online.mp4` (YouTube reads the file name).
2. Title, description, chapters and tags: copy from below. Title under 60 characters with the search phrase first.
3. Thumbnail: a still of the person from the hook plus 3-4 words in big type (green or cream panel, gold accent),
   readable at phone size. Ask vid-gen for `--still` frames; never use tiny UI as the thumbnail.
4. Captions: upload a proper English caption file (vid-gen can export SRT from `docs/vo/lines.txt` and the
   `vo` timings); don't rely on auto-captions.
5. Playlist, end screen (next tutorial + subscribe), card at the moment the blog post is mentioned.
6. **Altered or synthetic content: Yes** (our videos use generated people and places).
7. Audience: not made for kids. Category: Howto & Style (tutorials) or Science & Technology (product films).
8. After publishing: embed on the matching blog post (website team) and add the YouTube link to `Asset list.md`.
9. Short: upload the phone cut or reel separately, title ending in "#Shorts" is optional; link the full video
   with the "related video" field.

## SEO basics we follow

- The first line of the description repeats the search phrase naturally and says who it's for.
- Chapters (00:00 first, at least three) help Google show "key moments".
- 5-10 tags, mixing exact phrases and Nigerian place words. Tags matter less than title and description.
- Link to the blog post and the matching website page in the first three lines (with UTM tags).
- Same words people search: "service charge", "estate dues", "levy", "visitor pass", "gate pass",
  "estate management app", "Lagos", "Abuja".
