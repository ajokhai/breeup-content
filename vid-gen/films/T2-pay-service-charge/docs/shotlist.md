# T2 How to pay your service charge online

The source of truth is `film.js` (a `tutorial({...})` call on `tools/kit/tutorial.js`): every scene, time,
ring and caption is there, timed to the voice-over in `film.json` "vo". This file only records intent.

- Hook: generated photo of a resident paying on her phone; "Pay your service charge from your phone."
- Why: generated photo of a resident at his gate; "Your payment, recorded against your home, with a receipt."
- Intro card, then four steps on the real resident screens: Sign in, Open Bills (with the real desktop Bills
  row as a card), Transfer (account Copy, then the unit reference GE-D3), Or pay ahead (3 months / 1 year).
- Tip on a generated committee photo: estates rarely change their bank account; check before paying a new one.
- End card. Every scene sits on a photo (rule 4); the website's own photos are not used (rule 2).
