## Lessons
- Generative AI struggles specifically with hands interacting with objects (like holding a phone). Always check hands at 100% scale before approving an image, especially for full-frame portrait shots.
- Split-screen UI layouts still need to follow the background rules; a split design shouldn't introduce extended periods of flat color if the rules demand photo backgrounds.
- Always check the default transition settings in the motion engine; shape wipes can easily slip in if not explicitly overridden by the style guide's approved directional cuts.
