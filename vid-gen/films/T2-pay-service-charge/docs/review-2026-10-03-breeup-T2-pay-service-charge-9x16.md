## Lessons
1. **"Text behind subject" masking only works if it doesn't obscure the core message.** Never sacrifice the readability of your opening hook for a visual effect. When in doubt, put the text in front.
2. **Turn off discretionary ligatures for large headings.** Serif display fonts often contain old-style ligatures (like 'sf' or 'ct') that look sophisticated in print but register as glaring typos on screen.
3. **If you generate an AI image containing text, scrutinize it.** AI frequently hallucinates or misspells words (e.g., "COMMITEE"). Avoid generating text in props unless you plan to Photoshop it perfectly.
