Everyone who helps run the estate should have their own login, so each person sees only what they need.
Open Admins and roles in the admin sidebar, and click Add Admin User.
Enter their name and email. Leave the password blank, and BreeUp creates a temporary one.
Pick a role, like Estate Manager, Financial Officer or Security Officer.
Fine-tune each area to full access, read-only or no access, and allow exports only for people who need them.
Tip: fewer people with export rights means less resident data leaving the platform.
