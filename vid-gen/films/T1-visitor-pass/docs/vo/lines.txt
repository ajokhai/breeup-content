Expecting a guest, a delivery or an artisan? You can send them a gate pass on WhatsApp in under a minute.
First, open your estate's sign-in link, and sign in with your phone number and four-digit PIN.
On your Home page, find the Visitor gate pass card.
Type your visitor's name, choose the type of visit, then tap Create pass.
Now tap Send on WhatsApp. Your visitor gets their code in seconds.
At the gate, the guard checks the code. No phone calls to confirm, and the guard still has the final say.
