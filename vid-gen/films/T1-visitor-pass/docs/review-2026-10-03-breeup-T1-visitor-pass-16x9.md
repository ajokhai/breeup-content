## Lessons
1. **Avoid screens in AI generation:** Never prompt for an active device screen facing the camera. The AI will inevitably generate gibberish text and fake UI that ruins the shot. Prompt for the back of the phone or tablet.
2. **Check for specific actions, not just mood:** If the shot list calls for a specific prop interaction (like holding a phone for a match-cut), an image without it is useless, no matter how good the people look.
3. **Transitions matter:** A premium brand requires premium motion. Basic shape-scaling transitions feel cheap; use crisp, directional wipes or camera-like movements instead.
