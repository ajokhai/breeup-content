If your estate charges the same service charge every month, set it up once as a recurring bill. BreeUp bills every household on schedule.
Open Recurring bills in the admin sidebar, and click New bill.
Give it a name residents will recognise, like Service charge.
Enter the amount per household, choose how often it's charged, and pick the date of the first bill.
Tick Let residents pay ahead. They can cover several months at once, with an optional discount, like one month free for a year.
Click Save bill. Every household is billed on the first bill date, then every period after that.
Tip: a small discount for paying a year ahead means fewer people fall behind.
