Most estates already keep their residents in a spreadsheet. You don't need to retype it. Here's how to import it.
Open Households in the admin sidebar, then click Import CSV, and choose your file.
Check the preview. It shows how many rows are ready, and explains any that need fixing.
Click Import. Your households appear straight away.
Each household gets a temporary four-digit PIN, shown once, with a download so you can share them privately.
Tip: you can download the template first, and paste your data into it.
