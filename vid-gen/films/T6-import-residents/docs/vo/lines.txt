Most estates already keep their residents in a spreadsheet. With BreeUp, you can import everyone in minutes, including houses and landlords.
Go to Households in your admin sidebar, click Template to download our starter sheet, and open it.
Each row is one household. Unit and name are required. Use clear unit codes like street, plot and flat, for example S2-14A.
Use the house column to group flats in the same building. For example, units 14A and 14B both belong to house S2-14.
Add the landlord name and phone number. Write their name the same way on every unit they own, so BreeUp links them automatically.
Enter any current balance in naira without commas, then save your spreadsheet.
Click Import in Households, choose your spreadsheet, and check the preview. It verifies your rows and highlights anything to adjust.
Once imported, every household gets a temporary four-digit PIN. Download the list to share them securely with your residents.
Open the Houses and Landlords tabs to see counts at a glance, with units and property owners neatly organised.
If you already imported before, upload again with house and landlord columns. Existing units are updated, never duplicated.
