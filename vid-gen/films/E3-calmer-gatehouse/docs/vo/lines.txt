The gatehouse is where an estate's reputation is made. Long queues, calls to confirm every guest, and logbooks nobody can search.
With BreeUp, residents create a visitor pass in two taps, and send it on WhatsApp.
The guard checks the code on their phone. Every entry is timestamped and searchable.
There's no new hardware. It runs on the phones your residents and guards already carry.
And the guard still has the final say.
