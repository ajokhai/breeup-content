Invoices are how BreeUp knows what each household owes. Here's how to bill, record payments and send reminders.
Open Payments in the admin sidebar. The ledger shows every invoice, its status and its due date.
Click Generate Invoice, then choose All Households, or a single unit.
Enter the invoice type, the amount in naira and a due date. Add a note if residents need context.
Click Create Invoice. It appears in the ledger, and in each resident's portal.
When someone pays in cash or by direct transfer, click Settle to record it.
For anyone behind, click WhatsApp. It opens a ready-written reminder you can check before sending.
Tip: a short, personal message to one household works far better than a group broadcast.
