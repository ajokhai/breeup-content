# Device assets

- `iphone.glb`, `macbook.glb`: from the HyperFrames registry block `vfx-iphone-device` (HyperFrames, Apache-2.0).
- `studio.hdr`: "Studio Small 09" by Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09), CC0.
- `phone-frame.png`, `laptop-frame.png`, `frames.json`: rendered from the above by `tools/frames.mjs`.

A better phone model can replace `iphone.glb` (keep the screen mesh named `front-glass`), then rerun
`node tools/frames.mjs`. A CC-BY model needs its credit added here and in the video description.
