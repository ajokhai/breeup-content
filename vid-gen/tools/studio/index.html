How it works

  1. Paste your app's link and pick a style.
  2. Say what to show, like "sign up and create a project".
  3. Clipwalk makes it: it clicks through your app and adds words, a voice and music. Tidy it up if you like, then download.

Saw a video you love on X or TikTok? Pick "Like a video I saw" and paste its link. Yours will match its pace and feel.
