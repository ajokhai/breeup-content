# Start here

This folder holds everything BreeUp makes to promote itself. Five minutes with this page is enough to start.

**BreeUp** is estate management software for Nigerian residential communities: service charge billing and
online payments, WhatsApp visitor passes, notices, and AGM-ready records. Website: breeup.com.

## The three folders

| Folder | What's in it | Who opens it |
|---|---|---|
| **Final videos/** | Finished videos, ready to post. Names say what they are: `T2 How to pay your service charge online (phone).mp4`. "phone" = vertical (Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp status), "wide" = horizontal (YouTube, website). | Everyone |
| **Marketing/** | The plan: brand and messaging, social media, email outreach, ads, and a list of every video with what it's for. Start with `Marketing/README.md`. | Marketing (people or agents) |
| **vid-gen/** | The video workshop: scripts, generated photos, music, tools. Only needed if you make or change videos. Start with `vid-gen/README.md`, then `vid-gen/CLAUDE.md` (the rules). | Video makers |

Nothing else belongs at this level.

## If you are...

- **Posting or running ads:** read `Marketing/README.md`, pick a video from `Marketing/Asset list.md`, find the file in `Final videos/`.
- **Making videos:** read `vid-gen/CLAUDE.md` (rules, including Josh's taste) and the top of `vid-gen/HANDOFF.md`
  (who is working on what right now).
- **An AI agent:** same as above for your role. Coordinate with other agents only through `vid-gen/HANDOFF.md`
  (`git pull` first, push when done). Keep this structure: don't add files or folders at this level.
